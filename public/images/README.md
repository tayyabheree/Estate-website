## Cropping

- Hero: keep the house weighted to the right half so the left remains readable under the navy overlay.
- About: crop vertically at 4:5.
- Services: crop consistently at 4:3.
- Listings: crop consistently at 3:2.
- Appraisal: crop at 2:1 and keep detail restrained so white copy remains readable.
